About this game

The real South Pasadena, rebuilt for you and your friends.

Deliver the awkward stuff, get paid, and buy the town one shop at a time.

Features

  • Haul awkward cargoA five-tier cake. Corners matter.
  • Buy real shopsChipotle or the Rialto. Yours to run.
  • Trade with friendsSwap a house for a van at the credit union.
  • Become MayorOwn City Hall. Everyone calls you Mayor.
  • Titles and hatsEarn a title. Wear the hat.
  • Parachute outJump from anything. Land on Mission St.
  • Up to 12 friendsCrew jobs pay everyone more.
  • One shared townWhat you buy stays bought.
