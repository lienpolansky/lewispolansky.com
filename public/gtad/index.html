    01 About this game

    The real South Pasadena, rebuilt for you and your friends.

    Deliver the awkward stuff, get paid, and buy the town one shop at a time.

    This build: everyone starts with a white Pickup. Press Tab for a grid of everything you can do. Improve your home with a ramp, a garage, string lights, a front garden and a new coat of paint, and furnish the rooms inside. Choose one of 16 characters, then their colours and a hat. Dance with your friends. Up to 12 players online together, across Mission and Fair Oaks, 27 shops for sale and 12 free houses on Meridian Ave.
